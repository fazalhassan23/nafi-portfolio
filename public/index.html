Don't ignore these symptoms

If you notice any of these, get checked early.

  • Ear pain or discharge
  • Hearing loss
  • Blocked nose
  • Frequent sinus pain
  • Sore throat > 2 weeks
  • Hoarse voice
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Neck lump
  • Snoring & sleep apnea

FAQ

Questions patients often ask.

Short answers before you call. For anything urgent, contact the chamber directly.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form below, call, or message on WhatsApp. The chamber will confirm your time by phone.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Previous prescriptions, test reports (hearing test, X-ray, CT scan) and a list of any medicines you take.

Will I always need surgery?

No. Many ear, nose and throat problems are treated with medicine and follow-up. Surgery is advised only when it is needed.

When should I see an ENT doctor?

If ear pain or discharge, hearing loss, a blocked nose, a sore throat lasting more than two weeks, a hoarse voice or a neck lump does not settle.
