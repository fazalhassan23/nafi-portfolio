Clinical Expertise

ENT Conditions Treated

Specialist evaluation and management across the major clinical domains of otolaryngology, head, and neck care.

Ear & Hearing Disorders

Comprehensive assessment of acute and chronic ear infections, balance issues, and hearing problems in adults and children.

  • Acute & chronic otitis media (ear infections & ear discharge)
  • Hearing impairment & audiometric evaluation
  • Tinnitus (ringing, buzzing, or pulsation in the ear)
  • Vertigo, dizziness, and inner ear balance disorders
  • Eardrum perforation and middle ear fluid (glue ear)

Nasal & Sinus Conditions

Diagnostic endoscopy and medical or surgical relief for chronic nasal blockage, allergies, and sinus infections.

  • Chronic rhinosinusitis and facial pressure pain
  • Deviated nasal septum (DNS) causing airway obstruction
  • Allergic rhinitis and seasonal nasal allergies
  • Nasal polyps and turbinate hypertrophy
  • Recurrent nosebleeds (epistaxis) and smell disorders

Throat, Tonsil & Adenoids

Pediatric and adult care for persistent throat discomfort, recurrent tonsil infections, and obstructed nighttime breathing.

  • Recurrent acute and chronic tonsillitis
  • Adenoid enlargement in children causing mouth breathing
  • Snoring and obstructive sleep apnea evaluation
  • Pharyngitis and chronic sore throat sensations
  • Difficulty or discomfort during swallowing (dysphagia)

Voice & Head-Neck Assessments

Specialized vocal cord endoscopy and clinical evaluation of lumps or swellings in the neck and salivary glands.

  • Hoarseness and vocal changes lasting more than two weeks
  • Vocal cord nodules, polyps, and professional voice strain
  • Neck lumps and enlarged cervical lymph nodes
  • Salivary gland swelling (parotid and submandibular)
  • Thyroid nodule clinical assessment and coordination
Clinical Advice

When to Seek Timely ENT Evaluation

While many ear, nose, and throat symptoms are benign and resolve with routine care, certain clinical signs benefit from early specialist examination.

  • Voice hoarseness or alteration persisting beyond 2 weeks
  • Sudden or one-sided hearing loss or persistent ear drainage
  • Persistent, unexplained neck swelling or gland enlargement
  • Progressive difficulty or pain when swallowing food

Clinical Services

Surgical & Diagnostic Procedures

Confirmed clinical capabilities performed in equipped surgical facilities following complete clinical evaluation.

Diagnostic Endoscopy

Rigid and flexible video endoscopy of the nasal passages, nasopharynx, and larynx for direct, magnified visualization and precise diagnosis.

Tonsil & Adenoid Surgery

Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy performed for recurrent severe tonsillitis, obstructive sleep apnea, and chronic upper airway obstruction in children and adults.

Septoplasty & Airway Surgery

Corrective surgery for deviated nasal septum and enlarged turbinates to restore natural, unimpeded nasal breathing.

Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (FESS)

Minimally invasive endoscopic surgery for chronic, refractory sinusitis and nasal polyps, restoring sinus ventilation without external incisions.

Middle Ear Procedures

Grommet (ventilation tube) insertion for persistent middle ear fluid, and tympanoplasty for eardrum perforation repair where indicated.

Head & Neck Evaluation

Systematic clinical assessment, fine needle aspiration cytology (FNAC) coordination, and surgical biopsy of head and neck soft tissue swellings.

Clinical Treatment Principle: Procedural recommendations are made solely after in-person clinical assessment and relevant investigations. The majority of ear, nose, and throat conditions are managed effectively with conservative medical therapies and ongoing monitoring.
Patient Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful practical answers regarding consultations, chamber visits, and appointment scheduling.

How do I request and confirm an appointment? +

You can request an appointment using the form below, or by calling the chamber hotline directly. Once your request is submitted, our chamber reception desk will contact you by telephone to confirm your scheduled date and allocate your time slot.

What should I bring to my first consultation? +

Please bring any previous prescriptions, doctor notes, and diagnostic test reports—including audiograms (hearing tests), X-rays, CT scans, allergy panels, or blood work. A list of current medications is also helpful.

Does every ENT condition require surgery? +

No. Most ear, nose, and throat conditions are treated successfully with targeted medical therapy, lifestyle adjustments, and periodic follow-up. Surgery is only considered when medical treatment is insufficient or when structural problems require correction.

What should I do if I experience sudden or urgent symptoms? +

For urgent concerns during chamber hours, you can call the chamber desk for guidance. In the event of acute breathing distress, severe active bleeding, or acute trauma, please proceed immediately to the emergency department of the nearest hospital.

Can I book an appointment for a child or family member? +

Yes. Enter the patient's name in the name field and provide your contact mobile number. Pediatric ENT patients (ear infections, adenoids, tonsil enlargement) are routinely seen at the chamber.
