Conditions We Treat

Understanding your condition is the first step to recovery.

Plain-language information for patients and families about common ENT conditions.

👂

Ear Infections

Otitis media and externa, ear discharge, pain, and recurrent infections — expertly diagnosed and managed.

🔇

Hearing Loss & Tinnitus

Comprehensive evaluation of hearing problems, ringing in the ears, dizziness, and balance disorders.

Sinusitis & Allergy

Chronic sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, nasal polyps, and persistent blocked nose — thorough investigation and treatment.

Deviated Septum

Nasal obstruction, snoring and breathing difficulty caused by a deviated nasal septum.

Recurrent tonsillitis, adenoid problems, pharyngitis, and voice problems in adults and children.

🧣

Head & Neck Lumps

Thyroid and neck swellings assessed thoroughly with appropriate investigations and specialist referral.

FAQ

Questions patients often ask.

Short, clear answers. For anything urgent, contact the chamber directly.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form below, call, or message on WhatsApp. The chamber will confirm your time slot by phone within 24 hours.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Previous prescriptions, test reports (hearing test, X-ray, CT scan) and a complete list of any medicines you currently take.

Will I always need surgery?

No. Many ear, nose and throat problems are managed effectively with medicine and follow-up. Surgery is advised only when it is genuinely needed.

When should I see an ENT doctor?

If ear pain or discharge, hearing loss, a blocked nose, a sore throat lasting more than two weeks, a hoarse voice, or a neck lump does not improve — see a specialist.
